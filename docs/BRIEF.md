# Creative direction
Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. Audience: students of any college, branch and level. Action: assemble a three-person crew and register. The content file is authoritative for facts; blueprint is a storyboard. Prize pool INR 25,000. Unstop URL, fee basis, sponsor and organizer assets remain unconfirmed.

Industrial, photographic, tactical, cinematic. Gunmetal, bone, signal red. Barlow Condensed / IBM Plex Mono. Distinct operational chapters, hard cuts, menu jump points, quiet physical dossiers. No continuous flythrough.

Feeling curve: mystery (boot), scale (hero), trust (briefing), belonging (crew), pressure (breach), tension (45 to 10), urgency (escape), anticipation (locked vault), release (opening), ownership (ID), clarity (rules), resolve (final).

Peak: "I physically opened a vault by scrolling." Signature red route passes through scanner, selection grid, map, mechanical lock and timeline. Vault has largest pin span; quiet line precedes it.

Layers: photographic architectural ground, stable semantic typography, independent near-plane steel edge and document fragments, atmospheric detail. No duplicated extracted subjects. Mobile recomposes image crop; reduced motion removes pins and preserves content.

Score: parallax hero; natural-flow dossier with tilt; crew scanner; breach reveal; elimination count; lateral map pan; real-time 3D vault; loot flow; ID tool; rules disclosure; timeline. New workspace fingerprint registry has no previous rows. Operational chapter grammar fits better than a single film (jump navigation), catalogue or tool-only page (story), or purely editorial design (mechanical peak).

Media: hero v1 rejected for overly shadowed crew and intrusive foreground crate. Hero v2 accepted after visual inspection: recognizable red suits, vault lighting, negative space, coherent perspective. Briefing accepted after inspection for tactile props, matching grade and credible architectural detail. Original imagery is themed artwork.

Capability: no video-generation tool or KIE key; preflight also found no ffmpeg. A genuine Three.js mechanical vault substitutes for footage. No generated video is claimed. Unmodified Scroll Craft is the sole scroll clock.
